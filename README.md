## 📌 Project Overview

The **Coffee Shop Sales Analysis** project analyzes transactional sales data to uncover insights related to **orders, revenue, product performance, customer behavior, and time-based sales trends**.  
The project combines **Excel, SQL, and Power BI** to deliver an end-to-end business intelligence solution.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Analyze total orders, sales, and quantity sold  
- Identify best-selling products and categories  
- Understand sales trends by day, hour, and location  
- Compare current performance with last month (LM)  
- Support data-driven business decisions  

---

## 📝 Problem Statement

Coffee shop businesses generate large volumes of daily transaction data.  
To improve performance, businesses need to understand:

- Peak sales hours and high-revenue days  
- Product and category-wise performance  
- Location-based sales contribution  
- Weekday vs weekend sales behavior  
- Month-over-month sales trends  

This project addresses these needs using **SQL-driven analysis and Power BI dashboards**.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Analysis

### 🔹 Sales Performance
- Total Orders, Total Sales, Total Quantity Sold  
- Month-over-Month (MoM) sales comparison  
- Daily and hourly sales trends  

### 🔹 Product & Category Analysis
- Sales by product category  
- Sales by product type  
- Identification of top-performing items  

### 🔹 Time & Location Analysis
- Weekday vs weekend sales distribution  
- Sales by store location  
- Peak sales hours and days
